For students on Canvas · No extension needed

Homeroom

Canvas, redrawn as a clean classroom.

Class cards, a Stream, Classwork by topic, a To-do list and a Calendar, all built from your real Canvas classes. Turn in work and message your teacher without leaving it. It lives in a bookmark, so it works on school laptops that block extensions.

Homeroom

Set it up in a minute

  1. 1

    Show your bookmarks bar

    Press Ctrl Shift B on Windows or a Chromebook, or ⌘ Shift B on a Mac.

  2. 2

    Drag Homeroom onto it

    Grab the yellow button above and drop it on the bar. If that doesn’t work, use the copy option at the bottom of this page.

  3. 3

    Open Canvas and click it

    On any Canvas page, click Homeroom in the bar. Canvas turns into Homeroom right there in the tab.

What’s inside

  • Class cardsEvery class on your dashboard with its own banner art, teacher and what’s due next. Change a class’s color from the card menu.
  • StreamAnnouncements and new assignments, newest first, with an Upcoming box.
  • ClassworkYour modules laid out as topics. Click any item to see when it’s due, its status and a preview.
  • Turn in workAttach a file, a link or typed text and hit Turn in. Mark reading-type work as done.
  • Private commentsMessage your teacher right on the assignment.
  • Class commentsRead and reply under announcements and discussions, right from the Stream.
  • QuizzesSee questions, time limit and your attempts, then start the quiz without leaving Homeroom.
  • Pages and filesShown as class materials, with image previews and downloads.
  • To-doAssigned, Missing and Done across all your classes, grouped by week.
  • CalendarA week view of everything due, colored by class.
  • People and GradesYour teachers and classmates, plus every assignment’s score and your overall grade.
  • Light and darkSwitch from the account menu. Works on phones too.

Good to know

How the bookmark behaves

  • Moving around Homeroom stays in Homeroom, and the back button works.
  • Quizzes, discussions, pages, files and everything else open inside Homeroom. Pages it doesn’t redraw show Canvas’s own page inside Homeroom’s frame, with Canvas’s menus hidden.
  • Reloading the page brings regular Canvas back. Click the bookmark again to return to Homeroom.
  • Clicking the bookmark while Homeroom is open switches back to regular Canvas.

Private by design

  • Runs only in your Canvas tab and only talks to your school’s Canvas, using your normal login.
  • No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
  • It only changes things in Canvas when you do: turning in work, comments, “mark as done” and class colors.
  • Some school-managed Chromebooks block bookmarks like this too. If clicking does nothing, that’s why.
