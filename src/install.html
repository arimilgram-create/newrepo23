For students on Canvas

Quad. Canvas, upgraded.
No extension.

Dark mode, a real to-do list, grades on your dashboard cards and a GPA calculator. Quad lives in a bookmark, so it works on school laptops that block Chrome extensions, and in any browser that has a bookmarks bar.

Quad
Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. Don't click it here. It only runs on a Canvas page.

Set it up in a minute

  1. 1

    Show your bookmarks bar

    Press Ctrl Shift B on Windows or Chromebook, or ⌘ Shift B on a Mac.

  2. 2

    Drag Quad onto it

    Grab the yellow button above and drop it on the bar. If dragging doesn't work, use the copy option further down.

  3. 3

    Open Canvas and click it

    Go to your Canvas dashboard and click Quad in the bar. The panel opens on the right. Click again to hide it.

What you get

To-do list

Everything due in the next 7, 14 or 30 days, grouped by day, with overdue work pinned to the top. Check items off and they're marked done in your Canvas planner too.

Grades at a glance

Your current score and letter for every class in one list, and a badge with your grade on each dashboard card.

GPA calculator

Weighted and unweighted GPA on a 4.0 scale from your live grades. Set credits per class and mark Honors (+0.5) or AP/IB (+1.0).

Dark mode and cleaner cards

Darken all of Canvas, and swap card photos for solid course colors if you like a calmer dashboard.

Good to know

A bookmark, not an extension

Extensions can run on every page by themselves. A bookmark only runs when you click it, so:

  • Click Quad once after opening a Canvas page to bring back dark mode and card grades.
  • Your settings are remembered in this browser, separately for each Canvas site.
  • Some school-managed Chromebooks block bookmarklets too. If clicking does nothing, that's the reason.

Private by design

  • Quad runs inside your own Canvas tab and only talks to your Canvas site, using your normal login.
  • No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Nothing about your grades leaves your browser.
  • It reads your courses, grades and planner, and the only thing it changes is the "done" check on planner items.
